# Levels

The levels Kyle groups players into. A client's `level:` must be a slug from this
table. Starts with the ball colours; Kyle keeps, changes or replaces them at
first-run setup, and adds or removes one by chat.

| Level | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| `red-ball` | under 8, small court |
| `orange-ball` | 8–10, three-quarter court |
| `green-ball` | 9–11, full court, slower ball |
| `yellow-ball` | full court, full ball — juniors |
| `adult` | adult players |
