# Questionnaire: Ross only

What the coach will be asked at first-run setup. Going through it beforehand makes the first chat quick. The answers go
into the coach's Drive through their own Claude, never into this repo.

1. Which Google account and calendar do lessons go in?
2. What will the coaching folder in Google Drive be called?
3. Booking hours: weekdays, Saturday, Sunday.
4. Each court: name, where it is, anything useful (lights, access, parking).
5. Minutes of driving between courts, and the gap wanted between back-to-back lessons.
6. Prices: a private hour, other lengths, semi-private per player, any packages.
7. The cancellation and no-show policy, exactly as told to a parent.
8. Levels: keep the ball colours (red, orange, green, yellow, adult) or use their own?

For the Project instruction: the coach's first name, and the contact line the coach should use to reach you.
